## Brand & Style
This design system embodies high-velocity B2B institutional finance: authoritative, frictionless, and immaculately organized. It merges the institutional trustworthiness of tier-one enterprise banking with the refined, fluid precision of next-generation infrastructure software. 

The aesthetic is Modern High-Tech Corporate with subtle architectural elevation. It avoids the playfulness of consumer apps and the sterile clumsiness of legacy enterprise tools. Every element prioritizes dense, scannable data comprehension, optical balance, and deliberate visual rhythm. The emotional posture is crystalline confidence—providing operators, CFOs, and treasury leads the certainty needed to execute high-volume, multi-currency settlements with zero cognitive fatigue.

## Colors
The palette balances structural discipline with clear visual feedback:

- **Core Brand Anchor:** Midnight Obsidian (`#0b132b`) delivers architectural grounding across navigation sidebars, high-emphasis headers, and dominant interface anchors.
- **Action & Primary Elements:** Deep Electric Indigo (`#1d4ed8` resting, `#1e40af` active, with `#2563eb` for high-impact interactive highlights) commands focused interactions, selected states, and progressive disclosure flows.
- **Secondary Distinction:** Royal Violet (`#4f46e5`) introduces secondary analytical depth, distinguishing projection models and automated rule sets from direct transactions.
- **Positive Metrics & Liquidity:** Vibrant Sky/Cyan (`#0ea5e9` resting, `#38bdf8` light mode accents) signifies live liquidity, active reconciliation, and net-positive cash flows.
- **Surfaces & Canvases:** Clean slate-tinted neutral foundations (`#f8fafc` page canvas, `#f1f5f9` inset wells) contrast cleanly with stark white (`#ffffff`) modular cards.
- **Structural Boundary:** Fine micro-borders in crisp slate tones (`#e2e8f0` default, `#cbd5e1` active/hover) define separation without visual heaviness.
- **Semantic State Engine:**
  - *Settled / Cleared / Verified:* Emerald (`#059669` text/icon, `#ecfdf5` background, `#a7f3d0` border).
  - *Pending / Processing / Aging:* Amber (`#d97706` text/icon, `#fffbeb` background, `#fde68a` border).
  - *Overdue / Disputed / Flagged:* Crimson Rose (`#e11d48` text/icon, `#fff1f2` background, `#fecdd3` border).

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans serves as the singular typographic engine, engineered for exceptional structural clarity across complex financial layouts. 

- **Headlines:** Display scales feature tight negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) and deliberate line heights to maintain high impact and geometric composure without vertical bloat.
- **Body:** Neutral baseline tracking and strict vertical cadence maintain effortless legibility across transaction descriptions, ledger entries, and audit statements.
- **Tabular Numerals:** All numeric figures, monetary totals, exchange rates, and ledger outputs must activate the OpenType `tnum` (tabular figures) feature flag. This guarantees uniform column alignment across complex accounting tables and instant optical parity between debits and credits.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Form indicators, table column headers, and status badges utilize high-weight micro-scales (`11px` to `12px`) with expanded letter-spacing (`0.02em` to `0.03em`) and uppercase transformation where context demands instantaneous scanning.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid 12-column grid governed by an intentional 4px/8px incremental spacing scale, structured to preserve data integrity and information density:

- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12 columns with standard `1.5rem` (24px) gutters and `2rem` (32px) margins. Used for multi-pane cash flow management, dual-column account balancing, and wide financial analytics.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` (20px) gutters and `1.5rem` (24px) margins. Sidebars collapse to streamlined icon-rails; analytical panels stack progressively.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` (16px) gutters and `1rem` (16px) canvas margins. Complex tables shift gracefully into stacked transactional cards; horizontal metric rails scroll with snap points.

Internal component spacing follows strict proportional pairings: dense data rows utilize `space-xs` (4px) to `space-sm` (8px) internal gaps, while dashboard card modules employ `space-lg` (24px) padding to balance high information density with necessary negative space.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system is architectural and grounded, utilizing layered tonal surfaces combined with subtle, cool-tinted ambient shadows rather than dramatic artificial drops.

- **Level 0 (Floor/Canvas):** Neutral background tint (`#f8fafc`). Strictly flat, non-elevated baseline for dashboard grouping and master page wrappers.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Modules):** Pure white fill (`#ffffff`) encapsulated by a crisp 1px perimeter border (`#e2e8f0`). Elevated with a soft, multi-tier ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.02)`.
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Flyouts & Filter Menus):** Pure white fill (`#ffffff`) with border (`#cbd5e1`). Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Settlement Confirmations):** Overlaid on an institutional backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px); background-color: rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.5)`). Surface features shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.10), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.06)`.
- **Level Inset (Input Fields, Search Cavities & Data Wells):** Inset fill (`#f1f5f9`) with micro-recess: `inset 0 1px 2px 0 rgba(11, 19, 43, 0.05)` and subtle border (`#e2e8f0`).

## Shapes
The shape language implements precise geometry balanced by softened ergonomics (`roundedness: 2`). This profile provides immediate structural clarity while avoiding aggressive razor corners or overly playful bulbous forms.

- **Micro Components (8px / `0.5rem`):** Applied to buttons, inputs, segmented controls, chips, and table-row hover treatments.
- **Structural Containers (12px to 16px / `0.75rem` to `1rem`):** Applied to core content cards, data tables, metrics panels, and analytical charts.
- **Overlays & Modals (16px to 24px / `1rem` to `1.5rem`):** Applied to full-page dialogue sheets, modal viewports, and floating verification drawers.
- **Pills (Full Radius):** Reserved exclusively for semantic status badges, monetary change tags, and numerical count pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Electric Indigo (`#1d4ed8`) background with crisp white text (`#ffffff`), `0.5rem` (8px) radius, 1px transparent border, and a resting sub-shadow. On hover, shifts to `#1e40af` with subtle upward micro-translation. Focus renders an offset ring of `2px #93c5fd`.
- **Secondary:** Stark white background (`#ffffff`), 1px solid border (`#e2e8f0`), Midnight Obsidian typography (`#0b132b`). On hover, transitions to slate fill (`#f8fafc`) with border darkening to `#cbd5e1`.
- **Destructive:** Soft crimson tint fill (`#fff1f2`), 1px border (`#fecdd3`), Rose text (`#e11d48`). On hover, shifts to solid Rose fill (`#e11d48`) with stark white text.
- **Icon / Action:** Inset padding, zero baseline background, neutral slate hover state (`#f1f5f9`).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text & Currency Inputs:** Slate well fill (`#f8fafc`), 1px structural border (`#e2e8f0`), 8px radius. Active typing switches fill to `#ffffff` with a sharp focus ring in Electric Indigo (`#1d4ed8` outline with `0 0 0 3px rgba(29, 78, 216, 0.12)`). Monospaced or tabular numbers for currency inputs with sticky currency code prefixes (`USD`, `EUR`, `INR`) in muted slate (`#64748b`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px square or round profiles. Inactive: `#ffffff` fill with 1.5px border (`#cbd5e1`). Checked: Solid Electric Indigo (`#1d4ed8`) fill with sharp white check/dot icon. Focus states follow the primary focus ring.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Badges:** Fully rounded pills (`9999px`), `11px` typography with `font-weight: 600`, tracking `0.02em`.
  - *Settled:* Emerald `#ecfdf5` background, `#059669` text, `#a7f3d0` 1px border.
  - *Pending:* Amber `#fffbeb` background, `#d97706` text, `#fde68a` 1px border.
  - *Overdue:* Rose `#fff1f2` background, `#e11d48` text, `#fecdd3` 1px border.
- **Interactive Filter Chips:** 8px radius, `#ffffff` background, `#e2e8f0` border, `#0b132b` text. Active state adopts Indigo tint fill (`#eff6ff`) with `#1d4ed8` border and typography.

### Data Cards & Panels
- **Metric Cards:** White surface (`#ffffff`), 12px radius, 1px border (`#e2e8f0`), `space-lg` padding. Layout pairs an upper metadata label with an expansive headline figure using tabular numerals, accompanied by positive Sky/Cyan indicators or semantic delta badges.
- **Ledger Tables:** Enclosed in Level 1 card frames with flat headers (`#f8fafc`), upper-border dividers (`#e2e8f0`), and uppercase column tracking. Alternating row hovering employs subtle `#f8fafc` tint transitions without shifting layout geometry.

### Financial Domain Components
- **Currency Ticker Ribbon:** Inset slate strip (`#f1f5f9`) displaying live exchange rates, paired with microscopic color dots showing instantaneous liquidity spread.
- **Transaction Timeline Node:** Vertical sequence tracker utilizing 2px linking rails in `#e2e8f0` flanked by 24px radial status indicators (clearing checkpoints: initiated, risk verified, routed, settled).
